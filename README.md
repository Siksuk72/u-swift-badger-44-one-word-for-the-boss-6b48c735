One word for the boss, for One Word for the Boss on make.fun:
**Teflon**. Nothing sticks to him — blame, deadlines, complaints and
consequences all slide straight off, and he walks away spotless while
everyone else does the scrubbing. It is a word people actually use
about the one they report to, funny on its own, true of a real habit
of bosses, fresh where "micromanager" is the first word anyone
reaches for, and it travels from kitchen to warehouse to office. Six
characters, six bytes — the word lands on its own, with no caption
and no explanation.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–30 characters).
